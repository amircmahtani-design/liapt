DEMONSTRATION MEDIA
===================
The 157 photographs that ship with the app are already here, one per
movement, named after the exercise. index.html carries a baked-in map from
every exercise name to its file, so the app never guesses at a filename and
never spends a 404 looking for one.

WHAT IS HERE
    1586 x 992 (16:10) webp, roughly 38 KB each, 5.7 MB in total.
    The same model, kit and studio throughout; two positions, left to right,
    with an arrow between them. No text is baked into the pictures.
    They are fetched one at a time as each movement comes up, and the
    service worker keeps the ones she has seen for offline use.

STILL MISSING (12)

ADDING OR REPLACING ONE
    Name it after the exercise, lower case, hyphens for spaces and
    punctuation, and drop it in. Nothing needs registering.

        Hip Thrust                  ->  hip-thrust.webp
        Romanian Deadlift           ->  romanian-deadlift.webp
        90/90 Breathing             ->  90-90-breathing.webp
        Half-kneeling Pallof Press  ->  half-kneeling-pallof-press.webp

    To REPLACE a picture that already exists, use the same filename it has
    now. To ADD one for a movement that has none, any of jpg, webp, png, gif
    or mp4 works; an mp4 plays muted and loops.

    Settings -> Programme -> Demonstration pictures -> Check walks the whole
    catalogue and lists exactly which filenames are still missing.

SHAPE
    16:10 landscape, at least 1200 wide. The card crops to fill at 16:10 and
    thumbnails crop to the left, so keep the first position toward the left
    and the working body away from the very edges.
