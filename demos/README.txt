DEMONSTRATION MEDIA
===================
Drop the exercise pictures in this folder. Nothing needs registering
anywhere — the app looks for the file, and if it isn't here yet it shows a
labelled body map instead.

NAMING
    the exercise name, lower case, hyphens for spaces and punctuation

    Hip Thrust                  ->  hip-thrust.jpg
    Romanian Deadlift           ->  romanian-deadlift.jpg
    90/90 Breathing             ->  90-90-breathing.jpg
    Half-kneeling Pallof Press  ->  half-kneeling-pallof-press.jpg

Train-exercise-list.xlsx has the exact filename for every movement.

FORMATS
    jpg, webp, png, gif or mp4 — tried in that order, so a mixed folder is
    fine. An mp4 plays muted and loops.

SHAPE
    4:3 landscape, at least 1200 x 900. The app crops to fill, so keep the
    working body roughly centred with a little room at the edges.
